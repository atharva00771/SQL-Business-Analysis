## 📌 Project Overview

This project demonstrates how SQL can be used to analyze business data and
answer practical questions related to:

- 👥 Customer ordering behavior
- 🛒 Customer order frequency
- 📦 Product sales performance
- 🔥 Best-selling products
- 💰 Revenue generation

The goal is not only to write SQL queries, but also to understand the
**business problem behind the data and generate useful insights.**

---

## 🎯 Business Scenarios

### 👥 Customer Analysis

**1. Customer Order Analysis**

Find the total number of orders placed by each customer, including customers
who have never placed an order.

**2. Frequent Customer Analysis**

Identify customers who have placed more than 3 orders.

---

### 📦 Product Analysis

**3. Product Quantity Analysis**

Find the total quantity sold for each product.

**4. Top 5 Best-Selling Products**

Identify the top 5 products based on total quantity sold.

**5. Top 5 Revenue-Generating Products**

Identify the top 5 products generating the highest revenue.

**Revenue = Quantity Sold × Product Price**
